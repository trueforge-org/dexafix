Using the app

Getting started

DexaFix corrects smart-scale results with DEXA scan results. A smart scale is convenient but biased. A DEXA scan is accurate but rare. The app uses the difference between the two on the days you have both, and applies it to the scale readings in between. You get one estimated value per day.

  1. Create the first account. On a new server it becomes the administrator. After that, new accounts are closed until an administrator allows sign-ups (see Signing up).
  2. Enter your height under Settings (needed for BMI, FMI and LMI).
  3. Add one or more DEXA scans.
  4. Add scale readings by hand, by the JSON API, or by importing them from Withings. A DEXA scan only calibrates a scale if the scale has a reading on the same day.
  5. Open the estimates page to see the daily table, the chart and the calibrations in use.

Stored readings are never changed. Every estimate is calculated from them, so you can change settings at any time and see the result at once. Small ? badges throughout the app link to the matching section of this documentation.

The pages

Settings and Admin are in a menu under your first name (or your username, when you have not set one) in the header, which also has Sign out.

  • /: the home page for visitors: a short introduction next to the sign-in page (the sign-in page comes first on a phone). Sign in with an e-mail address and password, with a passkey (Sign in with a passkey), create an account, or use a provider button. A username is unique (ignoring case) and has 2 to 32 letters, digits, ., _ or -, starting with a letter or digit. You sign in with your e-mail address, not your username.
  • /u/{id}: redirects to the estimates page.
  • /u/{id}/estimates: one row per day with the estimated values, an overview with the latest values and a trend chart, the calibrations in use, and the Withings buttons.
  • /u/{id}/dexa: add and delete DEXA scans. A scan needs a date and at least one measurement.
  • /u/{id}/scale: add and delete readings. Times are in your time zone (Settings). Choose the measurement method in the drop-down and tick Athlete mode if it applies.
  • /u/{id}/settings: the athlete-mode default and the accuracy rank of each method. The ranking is a drag-to-reorder list, with up and down buttons for keyboard use. Without JavaScript it has rank number inputs instead. A Withings section connects, imports from and disconnects the user's Withings account. It says so when Withings credentials are missing, either on the Admin page or in the environment. Also:
    • Profile: username, first name and last name (both optional, up to 64 characters), e-mail, time zone and height in cm. The e-mail is required, because it is how you sign in; changing it asks for your current password. The time zone (an IANA name such as Europe/Amsterdam, default UTC) decides which calendar day a scale reading belongs to, how the date and time of a reading are read and shown, what "today" is and which day a Withings import covers. DEXA scans are plain dates and are not moved.
    • Access tokens: create and revoke personal access tokens for the API.
    • Linked accounts: link or unlink sign-in providers (unlinking asks for your current password, or a recent sign-in when you have none).
    • Two-step verification: turn it on or off, and replace your recovery codes. See Two-step verification.
    • Passkeys: add a passkey, rename it or remove it. See Passkeys.
    • Password: set or change the account's password. Changing it signs out the other sessions and revokes all access tokens.
  • /admin: administrators only. See The Admin page.

Numbers may use a decimal comma. Notes are limited to 500 characters.

While a scale reading or DEXA scan is being entered, an empty field shows a gray value when it can be calculated from what was typed. Weight, body fat %, fat mass and fat-free mass calculate from each other: any two give the rest. On the DEXA form, lean mass + bone mass gives fat-free mass. A typed value is never replaced. Gray values are saved with the form unless a value is typed instead. This needs JavaScript; without it the fields stay empty.

On the estimates page, a DEXA badge marks a value from a DEXA scan, and a corr. badge marks a corrected value. Faded values are interpolated or held, and a gray est tag in front of the date marks a row with a value interpolated between two readings of that field. Hovering a value shows its origin, the method and mode it is based on, and the raw reading. The calibrations section lists each scan's offsets (DEXA minus the reading on the scan day) per method and mode. Days with only a DEXA scan appear too. The daily table shows the same period as the chart (30 days, 90 days, 1 year or all time, 90 days by default). The Columns menu above the daily table shows and hides columns, and the choice is remembered per account.

Settings

Profile

Username (unique), first and last name (both optional), e-mail (required, it is how you sign in; changing it asks for your current password) and height in cm (50 to 280, optional). Height is needed for BMI, FMI and LMI.

Time zone

Your IANA time zone, such as Europe/Amsterdam (default UTC). It decides which calendar day a scale reading belongs to, how the date and time you type for a reading are read and shown, what "today" is, and which day a Withings import covers. DEXA scans are plain dates and are not moved by it. Changing it re-groups your readings into days, so the estimates are recalculated.

Password

Set or change your password (current password required when you have one). Shown only while password sign-in is on. Minimum 8 characters, at most 72 bytes. See Password sign-in.

Recent account activity

The latest security events on your account (time in UTC, event, who did it, detail, IP address). If you see something you do not recognise, change your password and revoke your access tokens.

The footer, terms and privacy

Every page has a footer with links to the documentation, the status page, the Terms of Service (/terms), the Privacy Policy (/privacy), the project's sponsor page on Open Collective and, as a GitHub icon, the source code (which the AGPL asks a server to offer). Signed-in people see a short, dismissible reminder above the estimates that the project lives on support; closing it is remembered for 90 days in a cookie.
