Withings and Strength Level

Withings

Each user links their own Withings account. Imported measurements are stored as scale readings with the source withings and the athlete-mode default from Settings. An administrator sets Withings up first: see Withings application and Withings workers and automatic imports.

Connecting and importing

  1. On the Settings or Estimates page choose Connect Withings and grant access. Finish within 10 minutes, the lifetime of the connection state.
  2. Choose Import from Withings. It fetches the whole history.

Connecting works only when the administrator has set the client id, secret and redirect URL; until then Settings shows Withings as not set up. Talking to Withings is a job for a worker, so imports wait while no worker runs. Automatic imports are off until an administrator gives them an interval; after the number of failed runs in a row that the administrator set, your automatic imports pause, until you disconnect and connect Withings again or an administrator resumes them.

Imported: weight, fat ratio, fat mass, fat-free mass, muscle mass, hydration, bone mass and visceral fat. Measurements Withings marks as possibly belonging to someone else (for example a guest) are ignored. Days are calendar days in your time zone.

Disconnect Withings forgets your tokens. Imported readings are kept.

Bulk import

Import from Withings fetches the whole history. Each day is handled by what is already stored:

Day has What happens
No readings Imported first.
Withings readings only Changed readings are updated, identical ones are left alone, new ones are added. Nothing deleted.
Manual readings New Withings readings are skipped and the day is listed in the result. Earlier imports are refreshed.

A manual entry is a deliberate value. Averaging it with a Withings reading of the same weigh-in would distort the day, so bulk import leaves such days alone.

Re-import one day

Use ↻ on a row of the estimates page, or ?day= on the API, to re-import one day. It replaces that day's Withings data:

  • Adds the day's Withings readings, even when manual readings exist.
  • Updates changed readings.
  • Removes stored Withings readings that Withings no longer returns, for example after a weigh-in was deleted in the Withings app.
  • Never touches manual readings.

Strength Level

Sends your estimated weight and body fat to your Strength Level account, so its strength standards use your real body weight.

  • Permission: an administrator must enable Strength Level sync for you on the Admin page (Strength Level permissions). Until then the card does not appear and the endpoints answer 403.
  • Login: your Strength Level e-mail and password, stored encrypted. Only e-mail and password accounts work, not Google, Apple or Facebook sign-in. The login is tested when you save it, so a wrong password is reported at once. The password is never shown again. Remove login deletes it.
  • Single day: the ⇪ button on a row of the Estimates page sends that day and replaces an entry that is already there.
  • Multi-day sync (needs the extra multi-day sync permission): choose Last N days (default 90, 1 to 3650). Days missing on Strength Level are added. With Also update days that already exist ticked, days that exist there with other values are overwritten too. Nothing is ever deleted. It runs in the background, one day at a time with a short pause, so long periods take a while; the progress shows on the page. Only one sync per user runs at a time.
  • What is sent: the daily estimate of weight and body fat, including interpolated and held days. Days without a weight estimate are left out.
