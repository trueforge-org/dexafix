Body composition, corrected

Your smart scale, corrected by your DEXA scan

A smart scale is convenient but biased. A DEXA scan is accurate but rare. DexaFix uses the difference between the two to correct the scale in between your scans.

  • Daily estimates of body fat, fat mass and fat-free mass
  • Import your readings from Withings and your lifts from Strength Level
  • Your readings and scans are encrypted with a key of their own
  • Free and open source
